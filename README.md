## Available skills

### Listing Writer + Fair Housing Checker

Writes MLS and marketing listing descriptions, then screens every line
against two separate bodies of law:

- **Fair Housing** — U.S. federal protected classes, common state and MLS
  additions, and Ontario Human Rights Code grounds. Flags the phrase,
  names the protected class, suggests a compliant rewrite.
- **Licensee advertising rules** — state real estate commission and
  provincial regulator requirements, plus MLS public-remarks rules. A
  listing can pass a Fair Housing screen and still breach these.

## Installing

1. In Claude, go to **Settings → Capabilities** and turn on
   "Code execution and file creation."
2. Go to **Customize → Skills**, click **+**, and upload the ZIP exactly
   as downloaded. Don't unzip it first.
3. Toggle the skill on.

Works on Claude Free, Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise.

Full setup guide and troubleshooting: [Setup & troubleshooting](https://join.trevormchaffie.com/skill-help)

## Disclaimer

These are automated drafting aids, not legal advice. You, your brokerage,
and your MLS remain responsible for Fair Housing and advertising
compliance. Terms are context-dependent and rules vary by jurisdiction.
When in doubt, consult your broker or legal counsel.
